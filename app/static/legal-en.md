# Privacy Policy

KK AI助手 is operated independently by the site owner and is not affiliated with xAI, OpenAI, or Google. This page is a product template. Last updated: **2026-08-18**.

## What we process

- **Accounts**: username, password hash (not the raw password), and sign-up time.
- **Chats and files**: conversations, uploads, and generated images are stored on the operator’s server.
- **Cookies**: `kk_user` (signed login session, about 30 days, HttpOnly), `kk_vid` (visitor id for rate limits), and the admin token cookie `kk_token` (a hash of the admin password, not the password itself).

We do not sell your personal data.

## Storage and retention

Chat and session data stay until you delete the session or the operator purges data for capacity, safety, or maintenance. Uploads are cleaned up on a default **7-day** TTL (`KK_UPLOAD_TTL_SEC` can change this). Generated images stay on the server until deleted or purged.

## Third-party processing

To generate replies, messages are sent to the third-party model relay **CCAPI** and may be processed by upstream model providers under their own policies. The operator cannot control how those providers retain or use that content.

## When the operator can access data

The operator can access data on the server to run the service, fix issues, keep it safe, and handle abuse. That is not a sale or a public disclosure of your chats.
